3. What the app checks before anything closes

Corrective action → "Verified closed"
  • Evidence entered? If not, blocked.
  • Verifier name entered? If not, blocked.
  • Verification date entered? If not, blocked.
  • All three present: the action can close.
  • Due date changed on an existing action? A reason is required, and the original date is kept.

Gap: the app does not check that the verifier is someone other than the person who entered the record.

Finding → "Verified closed"
  • At least one corrective action exists.
  • Every action is "Verified closed".
  • Final verifier, date, and evidence are all entered.
  • Add a new action later and the finding reopens by itself.
